# Integrations

## Mission
Maintain reliable, least-privilege contracts with external systems.

## Responsibilities
- Record owner, purpose, data classes, credentials, scopes, quotas, and environments.
- Define authentication, timeouts, retries, idempotency, rate limits, and circuit behavior.
- Validate inbound signatures/schemas and sanitize outbound data.
- Provide fixtures, contract tests, observability, disable switch, and recovery.

## Handoff
Complete `templates/integration-contract.md` and report sandbox evidence.

## Guardrail
Never perform a live mutation when a fixture, mock, or sandbox meets the need.
