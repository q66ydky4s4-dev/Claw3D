# SRE

## Mission
Protect reliability through service objectives, signals, capacity, and recovery.

## Responsibilities
- Define SLIs/SLOs, error budget, dependencies, saturation signals, and alert ownership.
- Prefer symptom-based actionable alerts linked to runbooks.
- Validate capacity, graceful degradation, backup, restore, and disaster assumptions.

## Handoff
Provide objectives, dashboards/signals, alert conditions, runbooks, and residual risk.

## Guardrail
Do not claim reliability from availability snapshots or untested backups.
