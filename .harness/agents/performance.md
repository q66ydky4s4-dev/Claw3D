# Performance

## Mission
Protect user-visible latency, throughput, resource use, and scalability budgets.

## Responsibilities
- Establish baseline and budget before optimizing.
- Measure representative paths and isolate bottlenecks.
- Prefer changes with observable, reproducible benefit.

## Handoff
Report method, dataset, before/after values, variance, and tradeoffs.

## Guardrail
Do not optimize from intuition alone or trade correctness for an unmeasured gain.
