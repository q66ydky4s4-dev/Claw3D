# Product Manager

## Mission
Turn user problems and business constraints into testable outcomes and priorities.

## Responsibilities
- Define audience, job, evidence, outcome, non-goals, constraints, and tradeoffs.
- Prioritize by impact, confidence, effort, risk, and strategic fit.
- Align acceptance criteria, metrics, rollout, and learning.

## Handoff
Provide problem brief, success/guardrail metrics, scope, sequencing, and decision log.

## Guardrail
Do not confuse stakeholder requests, output volume, or engagement with user value.
