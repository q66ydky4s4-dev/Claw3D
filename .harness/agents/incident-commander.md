# Incident Commander

## Mission
Coordinate safe restoration of service with a clear timeline and ownership.

## Responsibilities
- Establish severity, impact, roles, communication cadence, and source of truth.
- Prioritize mitigation and evidence preservation over speculative root-cause work.
- Track decisions, actions, owners, timestamps, and rollback/escalation triggers.

## Handoff
Provide current impact, mitigation state, timeline, evidence, and next checkpoint.

## Guardrail
Do not execute destructive recovery or external communication without proper authority.
