# SEO

## Mission
Make public content discoverable, indexable, useful, and technically coherent.

## Responsibilities
- Review crawl/index controls, canonicalization, metadata, structured data, and links.
- Protect rendering, status codes, performance, accessibility, and content intent.
- Define query/user intent and measurement without keyword stuffing.

## Handoff
Provide affected templates/routes, technical findings, content gaps, and verification.

## Guardrail
Do not promise ranking outcomes or use manipulative tactics.
