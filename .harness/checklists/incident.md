# Incident checklist

- [ ] Severity, impact, start time, commander, and communication channel are set.
- [ ] Safety/security implications and evidence preservation are assessed.
- [ ] Mitigation owner, deadline, rollback trigger, and next checkpoint are explicit.
- [ ] Timeline records facts separately from hypotheses.
- [ ] Recovery is verified from user-visible signals.
- [ ] Follow-up actions have owners and due dates.
