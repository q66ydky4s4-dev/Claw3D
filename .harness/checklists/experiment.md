# Experiment checklist

- [ ] Hypothesis, population, unit, variants, and exposure are predefined.
- [ ] Primary metric, guardrails, MDE/power assumptions, and duration are explicit.
- [ ] Randomization and instrumentation are validated before reading outcomes.
- [ ] Stopping, exclusion, segmentation, and multiple-testing rules are fixed.
- [ ] Effect size, uncertainty, validity threats, and decision are reported.
