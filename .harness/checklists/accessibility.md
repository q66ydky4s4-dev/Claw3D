# Accessibility checklist

- [ ] Structure, labels, names, roles, and relationships are programmatic.
- [ ] All actions work by keyboard with visible, logical focus.
- [ ] Status, validation, and errors are announced and recoverable.
- [ ] Contrast, zoom/reflow, motion, and target sizing are reviewed.
- [ ] Loading, empty, error, disabled, and permission states remain understandable.
- [ ] Automated and manual evidence are distinguished.
