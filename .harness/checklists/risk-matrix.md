# Risk matrix

- [ ] Changed behaviors and affected users are listed.
- [ ] Failure severity, likelihood, detectability, and reversibility are rated.
- [ ] Critical data, money, identity, permissions, and external effects are flagged.
- [ ] Each material risk maps to a prevention or detection check.
- [ ] Test layer, environment, fixture, command, and owner are explicit.
- [ ] Skipped checks include reason, residual risk, and follow-up owner.
