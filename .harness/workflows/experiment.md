# Experiment workflow

1. Experimenter preregisters hypothesis, design, metrics, duration, and decision rule.
2. Analytics validates assignment, exposure, and event quality.
3. Implementer releases behind a reversible control; QA runs an A/A or equivalent check.
4. Analyze effect and uncertainty after the stopping rule; record decision and learning.

## Gate
Experiment checklist passes and no outcome is read before instrumentation validation.
