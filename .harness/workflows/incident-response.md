# Incident response workflow

1. Incident Commander sets severity, impact, roles, cadence, and source of truth.
2. SRE gathers signals; Security joins for suspected abuse or exposure.
3. Choose the safest bounded mitigation and preserve evidence.
4. Verify recovery from user-visible signals and continue monitoring.
5. Produce an incident report and owned corrective actions.

## Gate
Service recovery is verified; unresolved safety risk is explicitly escalated.
